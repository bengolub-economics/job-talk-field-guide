The examples

Nine cases use public research talks, spanning trade, labor, health insurance, banking, macro finance, development finance, climate, and network games. A tenth, constructed case compares numbered and mnemonic notation for two forecasts. Public-source slides retain their original appearance; every revised slide is rebuilt in the gallery’s own style. The constructed notation case uses identical layouts on both sides so that only the symbols change. Cases with several original pages identify any omitted builds or intervening slides.

The two-player calculation is an exact specialization of its source model. Reported numbers and calculated examples are distinguished in each case’s reconstruction notes. The banking coefficient plot adds approximate 95% intervals calculated as estimate ± 1.96 × reported standard error. The carbon-tax chart is digitized from its published figure.

The source search

The initial search replaced six constructed cases with public decks; a second pass diversified the subjects and replaced the lecture sources with research presentations. The search notes record the selected examples, neighboring-slide checks, and alternatives that did not support the same diagnosis.
