Reworks before

A listener can follow the calculation yet forget what x represents or whether 1 means market or survey. The revision uses f for forecast, π for precision, and subscripts M and S for the sources. These reminders remain useful in both the averaging formula and the later derivative. Attach the same source label to its forecast and its precision, and keep the definitions on the slide. Both versions here use the same content and layout; the change is the choice of symbols.
