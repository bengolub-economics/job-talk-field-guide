Draw the uncertainty with the estimate.

Display uncertainty alongside the estimates you discuss. A listener should be able to see both the estimated magnitude and how precisely it is measured.

Reworks before

Interpreting an estimate requires information about its precision. Plotting estimates with clearly labeled confidence intervals lets the audience see their magnitudes and uncertainty together, with the relevant reference value marked. Include imprecise estimates when they belong to the comparison; they also matter for what the evidence establishes.
