02 / Macro finance / HIDDEN QUESTION

Explain the question before the model.

Introduce a model by explaining the economic question it will answer. The audience can then understand why its assumptions and equations are needed.

Reworks before

Before introducing a model’s formal structure, explain the behavior or outcome you want to understand and the difficulty the model will help resolve. As you introduce agents, constraints, and choices, connect them to that purpose. This gives listeners a reason to follow the setup and a way to judge which details matter.
