Reworks before

Listeners may forget a definition or miss the moment when it is introduced. Put short definitions beside the expressions that depend on them, even if the notation appeared earlier. State what the calculation measures or what the equation says about economic behavior, so the audience can recover the argument while looking at the current slide.
