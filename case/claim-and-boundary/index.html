The Macroeconomic Impact of Europe’s Carbon Taxes ↗
Gilbert E. Metcalf, James H. Stock · 16 July 2020 · PDF 25, 40

Values and reconstruction

The GDP-level response is replotted from the chart on PDF p. 25. Coordinates were digitized from its raster plot and are approximate.

The plot retains horizons 0–6, both 67% and 95% bands, and the zero line. The vertical axis is tightened to ±3 percentage points so the bands can be read.

The experiment is a $40 carbon-tax increase in real 2018 US dollars, weighted by coverage share. Sample EU+; restricted local projections; parallel-path assumption; four lags.

Approximate point estimates at years 0–6: 0.04, 0.13, 0.31, 0.29, −0.04, 0.08, 0.08 percentage points.
