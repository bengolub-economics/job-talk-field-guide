A limitation is informative when the audience understands its consequence for a claim. State the finding, identify the inference at issue, and explain how a remaining uncertainty or assumption restricts that inference. Distinguish questions about causal interpretation, precision, and applicability to other settings, so listeners understand both what the work establishes and what remains open.
