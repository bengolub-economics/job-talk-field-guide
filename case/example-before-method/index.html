Values and reconstruction

Source parameters: F=1,D=$350,C=0.2,S=$2,100.

At $1,000 total spending,patient payment=$350+0.2×$650=$480; insurer payment=$520.

The cap starts at total spending $350+($2,100−$350)/0.2=$9,100.

P(Q) is a newly introduced notation; A+P(Q)=Y−m is equivalent to the source’s budget.

The illustrative bill and plotted schedule are computed from the contract,not observed or estimated outcomes.
