Reworks before

A results slide is easier to follow when listeners know which conclusion its evidence is meant to support. State that conclusion in a sentence at the top, then use the table or figure to substantiate it. Keep the wording precise about the population, comparison, and strength of inference, including whether the evidence establishes an association or a causal effect.
