Values and reconstruction

Aid (% GDP): haven 3.391 (1.154); non-haven −1.511 (1.035); ratio 4.973 (1.715).

GDP (% growth): haven 0.122 (0.141); non-haven 0.147 (0.063); ratio −0.001 (0.150).

All columns include country and time fixed effects. Standard errors clustered by country.

Observations: 1,648 / 1,652 / 1,645. R-squared: 0.101 / 0.092 / 0.076.

The dependent variable is quarterly growth in log deposits. A one-percentage-point increase in aid/GDP is associated with 3.391 log-percentage-points higher haven-deposit growth, controlling for GDP growth and country and time fixed effects.
