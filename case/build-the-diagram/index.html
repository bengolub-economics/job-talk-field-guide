A dense flow diagram asks the audience to find its own route through the argument. Begin with one payer and one recipient, then add the return flows and the next sector while keeping the familiar objects in place. Here, that means consumption first, wages and profits next, and government payments after that. The complete accounts can remain available for reference; they need not be decoded all at once.
