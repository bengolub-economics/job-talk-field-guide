The case 11 source has seven computational blocks, twenty labeled directed dependencies, three unknown paths, and three equilibrium targets. The last better build is the unchanged original graph. Its complexity is now introduced through selected routes rather than reduced by deleting connections.

Two diagnoses became more precise during the search. Case 10 is an opportunity to shorten the route to detailed evidence; the source already previews findings early. Case 12 concerns a closing slide whose caveats can be attached more explicitly to a finding; those caveats do not precede the talk’s results.

## Three full outlines

**Case 04: Athey–Imbens, NBER machine learning lecture.** Keep the survey’s topic coverage. Open each method chapter with a concrete task and example, then explain its mechanism and check the comparison. The source already introduces prediction and validation; the new outline develops that approach into a recurring chapter structure.

**Case 09: HANK.** Keep the original order and strong early findings preview. Add bridges into solution, parameterization, the policy experiment, and variants. Group PDF 21–26 as one progressive decomposition. Keep supporting pages 34–41 separate from the main route.

**Case 10: Competitive Search.** Preserve the early answer on PDF 4. The proposed six-stage core runs through the question, restrictions, observed and estimated objects, tests, fit, and conclusion. The full likelihood is an optional branch from the estimated-object map, with a return to the tests. The source’s own return-linked reserve tables remain available.

The outlines summarize source blocks; they are not facsimiles of original slides. Every source block links to its PDF pages. Proposed stages record their source material, and new synthesis is identified in the description. Page counts are not converted into invented speaking times.

## Checks applied to the published revisions

- Compared original pages and neighboring builds before selecting a case.
- Preserved all numbers, standard errors, significance marks, and sample details in the aid table.
- Verified the two-player equilibrium: both actions equal 4/3; the response matrix has spectral radius 1/4. The expansion counts repeated-link walks and is not a timeline.
- Transcribed twenty HANK dependencies and checked household outputs against the authors’ code. Kept both direct feeds into the equilibrium conditions and returned to the original graph.
- Preserved both 67% and 95% carbon-tax confidence bands, the 0–6-year horizon, and the distinction between GDP levels and GDP growth.
- Grouped animation builds, separated reserve slides, and preserved early result previews in the outline comparisons.
- Kept the timing exercise explicitly separate as a constructed practice scenario.
