THE ADVICE BEHIND THE EXAMPLES

Design for the person
trying to follow.

The essay’s advice is about understanding under limited attention. These are the decisions the gallery makes visible.

01

Give attention a reason to stay.

The listener is choosing whether to follow. A specialist may fill in what you leave unsaid; the persuadable economist outside your field often cannot. Make the question and the contribution understandable before the machinery.

  • 02 / Give the model a reason to exist. →
02

Say the point before the detail.

A model should arrive with a purpose; a results slide with a finding. The details then answer a question the audience already has. A clear title must still respect what the evidence establishes.

  • 07 / Put the finding in the title. →
  • 12 / Attach each limitation to the result. →
03

Help memory where it fails.

A definition seven slides ago is not a definition on this slide. Keep meanings near the symbols. At section breaks, restore the argument, not just its outline.

  • 03 / Put the meaning next to the symbol. →
  • 09 / Restore the argument at each stop. →
04

Build from a case the room can understand.

Give the audience a small working example before the general statement. Explain exactly what carries over, and retain the conditions the general result needs.

  • 04 / Give the model a concrete case first. →
  • 08 / Let two people teach the general case. →
05

Give a picture time to become familiar.

Complexity is not always a defect. It matters how much arrives at once and how long the room has to absorb it. Teach each piece, then let the whole picture do its work.

  • 05 / Explain one segment before the full diagram. →
  • 11 / Teach one path before the full system. →
06

Make the evidence answer a visible question.

A table preserves values but can hide the comparison. Draw the comparison you need, keep its units and uncertainty, and make the complete source available. Do not improve the headline by discarding a troublesome result.

  • 01 / Make the comparison visible. →
  • 06 / Draw the uncertainty with the estimate. →
07

Prepare a core that can survive questions.

For a long job talk, plan roughly twenty distinct core displays. An animation build is not another display. Aim to finish the introduction around ten minutes, reach main results by thirty to thirty-five, and convey the essential contribution by sixty. Rehearse sections that can expand or contract.

  • 10 / Give the evidence a main route. →
08

Own the limits as well as the result.

The point is to present yourself as a credible researcher. A concrete account of what the paper establishes, where it stops, and what the next paper would need is stronger than either an overclaim or a list of apologies.
