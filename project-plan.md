# Job talk field guide: project plan

Source baseline: Benjamin Golub’s `job-talk-essay.md` and `job-talk-essay-revised.md`, both last modified September 13, 2026; the supplied talk and slide-design materials. The current integrated essay is the reading companion; the revised draft supplies the parallel Q&A context.

## Purpose and audience
Give economics job-market candidates a way to recognize communication failures in their own decks and see an actual repair. Each case starts with a visible artifact. The unit of evaluation is what an economist outside the subfield can understand and retain.

## Research and evidence
1. Extract failure modes from the current essays, retaining their distinction between slide design, argument structure, and time management.
2. Search publicly accessible primary-source research decks. Verify selected slides against rendered pages and use one-based PDF page references.
3. Prefer examples whose problem can be established from the artifact. A dense page is not proof of poor live delivery; overlays, audience, and format matter.
4. Construct clearly labeled examples for modes that cannot fairly be inferred from a public deck. Never attach invented results or timing to a real author.
5. Keep a source ledger with URLs, pages, retained numbers/assumptions, transformations, and limitations. Separate original, adapted, and constructed artifacts visibly.

## Case architecture
Each case has: failure mode; slide or whole-talk scale; visible before; a fully authored better version; a listener-level diagnosis; concrete changes; what the revision preserves or defers; a short speaker cue; an essay section link; and provenance. Animated cases allow deliberate step-through; macro cases show actual slide sequences or time allocations.

## Product and design
An editorial field guide: prominent paired slide artifacts, confident typography, ink and vermilion, restrained teal for revised content, clear numbering and a concise index. The gallery appears immediately. Visitors can browse single-slide and whole-talk cases, inspect comparisons, step through progressive reveals, and read the relevant essay. Mobile layouts stack comparisons and keep labels attached. Keyboard focus and reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Division of work
- Research agent: locate, verify, and propose faithful public-source slide repairs.
- Architecture agent: extract the essay taxonomy and author whole-talk teaching cases; later critique coverage and fidelity.
- Design critic: propose visual/interaction design and inspect the implementation for clarity, accessibility, and editorial integrity.
- Lead agent: build the site, integrate evidence and assets, construct revised examples, resolve critiques, validate, and publish.

## Review and revision gates
- Is the diagnosis actually visible in the before?
- Does the better change the reader’s inference, rather than just the decoration?
- Are all reported numbers, units, uncertainty, assumptions, and scope preserved or explicitly accounted for?
- Are teaching inventions unmistakably labeled?
- Can the listener explain the point without a glossary from seven slides ago?
- Does the whole-talk material teach a usable route through the argument and a credible way to shorten it?
- Do navigation, routes, assets, source links, and build checks succeed?

Resolve substantive critic feedback before final publication. Publish privately to the user’s Sites account and deliver the working website with the project’s source and provenance preserved locally. No emails or other outbound messages.
